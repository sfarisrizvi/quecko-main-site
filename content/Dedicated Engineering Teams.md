Our process has four steps: consultation, talent matching, onboarding, and ongoing support. We identify your needs, match you with top-tier developers from our 1,500+ engineers, onboard them onto your tools, and provide continuous support to ensure they integrate seamlessly.

**Q2. How fast can Quecko staff and onboard engineers into our team?**
Typically, we match and onboard engineers within 7 to 14 days, depending on the complexity of the skills required.

**Q3. Who manages the dedicated engineers on a daily basis?**
They work directly under your management, using your own Slack, Jira, and GitHub workflows, so they feel like a natural extension of your internal team.

**Q4. What happens if an engineer isn't a good fit for our team?**
We offer a talent matching replacement process. If an engineer's performance does not meet expectations, we will identify and onboard a suitable replacement quickly.
