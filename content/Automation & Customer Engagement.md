## 10. OBJECTION HANDLING / FAQ

**Q1. What automation and customer engagement services does Quecko offer?**
Quecko specializes in building custom WhatsApp automation flows, AI chatbots, customer support ticket automation, marketing campaign triggers, lead generation and qualification systems, sales automation sequences, Telegram group bots, custom API workflow integrations, and omnichannel communication hubs.

**Q2. Do we need custom automation if we already use tools like Zapier or Make?**
Zapier is useful for simple tasks, but as sync volumes scale, subscription fees grow exponentially. Custom integrations avoid rate limits, eliminate third-party data processing risks, support highly complex multi-stage logic, and save on subscription overhead.

**Q3. Can Quecko automate messaging on official WhatsApp and Telegram APIs?**
Yes. We set up official business messaging API endpoints, design and register message templates, and build automated conversational logic to engage customers securely and within platform compliance.

**Q4. How does the resource augmentation process work for automation engineers?**
We consult with your team to understand your software stack, match you with top-tier database or backend engineers, onboard them seamlessly, and support them as they scale your development capacity.

**Q5. How do we get started with Quecko?**
Getting started is simple. Contact us via our website or email (Sales@Quecko.com). We will schedule a technical consultation to map out your requirements and recommend the best roadmap for your project.
