## 12. FAQs

**Do you provide formal legal opinions or do you act as securities counsel?**
We operate as consultants mapping out technical and regulatory strategy across jurisdictions; we collaborate directly with your locally licensed legal counsel or introduce vetted securities lawyers for formal legal opinions, since Quecko itself isn't a licensed law firm in any jurisdiction and is transparent about that distinction throughout the engagement.

**Which jurisdictions does your advisory coverage include?**
We regularly support projects launching in the US, European Union (under MiCA), UAE (under VARA), Singapore (under MAS), Hong Kong, and offshore hubs including the Cayman Islands and BVI, with jurisdiction-specific legal partners engaged as needed for formal filings.

**What is the Travel Rule and how does it affect our platform?**
The Travel Rule (FATF Recommendation 16) requires virtual asset service providers to collect and share originator and beneficiary information on qualifying transactions above jurisdiction-specific thresholds. We assess whether and how it applies to your specific platform and integrate the data-sharing protocols needed to comply, since this obligation can apply even to platforms that consider themselves purely domestic.

**How do you reconcile data privacy (GDPR) with AML data collection requirements?**
We design data governance workflows that minimize retention periods where possible, enforce appropriate local database hosting, and apply privacy-by-design principles to limit unnecessary cross-border data exposure — since AML's data-collection mandate and GDPR's data-minimization principle genuinely pull in different directions and need deliberate reconciliation, not just a policy document asserting both are satisfied.

**Does token classification affect how our project is taxed?**
Yes. Whether your token is treated as a commodity, security, or utility currency for tax purposes typically follows its broader legal classification, which influences cost-basis accounting treatment and tax nexus determination — we coordinate this analysis jointly with our tax advisory work rather than treating classification and taxation as unrelated questions.

**Can you help our institutional risk committee write internal policies?**
Yes. We draft internal institutional crypto governance frameworks, prepare risk briefing materials for board and committee review, and assist in structuring controlled pilot programs that satisfy an institution's existing risk appetite and approval processes before broader crypto adoption is considered.
