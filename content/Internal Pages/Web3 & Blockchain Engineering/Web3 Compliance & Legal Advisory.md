## 12. FAQs

**Do you provide formal legal opinions or do you act as securities counsel?**
We operate as consultants mapping out technical and regulatory strategies; we collaborate with your locally licensed legal counsel or introduce security lawyers for formal opinions.

**Which jurisdictions does your advisory coverage include?**
We regularly support projects launching in the US, European Union (under MiCA), UAE (under VARA), Singapore (under MAS), Hong Kong, and offshore hubs (Cayman Islands, BVI).

**What is the Travel Rule and how does it affect our platform?**
The Travel Rule requires virtual asset service providers (VASPs) to share originator and beneficiary data on qualifying transactions. We integrate the protocols needed to comply.

**How do you reconcile data privacy (GDPR) with AML data collection requirements?**
We design data governance workflows that minimize storage times, enforce local database hosting, and use privacy-by-design rules to limit cross-border exposure.

**Does token classification affect how our project is taxed?**
Yes. Whether your token is treated as a commodity, security, or utility currency influences cost-basis accounting and Nexus. We guide planning around this.

**Can you help our institutional risk committee write internal policies?**
Yes. We draft institutional crypto governance frameworks, prepare risk brief decks, and assist boards in setting up controlled pilots.
