## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Remittance corridors like the UAE-to-South Asia route move tens of billions of dollars a year, and expatriate-heavy markets are actively piloting digital settlement rails to cut costs — the UAE's own CBDC roadmap explicitly cites remittance cost reduction as a core goal. Stablecoins offer that same cost and speed advantage today, without waiting for a government-issued digital currency.

### Body
Traditional remittance still routes through multiple correspondent banks, each adding delay, fees, and FX spread. A stablecoin remittance platform has to replace that chain end-to-end — sender onboarding, compliant transfer, and a reliable local payout — without losing the trust senders place in familiar cash-pickup and bank-deposit options.

## 12. FAQs

**Which corridors can you support?**
Any corridor with viable local on/off-ramp partners — we help identify and integrate them during the corridor mapping phase.

**How is compliance handled per country?**
Our Compliance & Legal Advisory team designs jurisdiction-specific AML/CFT and licensing frameworks alongside the build.

**Do recipients need a crypto wallet?**
No — the platform handles conversion so recipients receive local currency via bank, mobile wallet, or cash pickup, with no crypto knowledge required.

**How much faster is this than traditional remittance?**
Settlement typically completes in seconds to minutes rather than the 1–3 days common with correspondent banking routes.
