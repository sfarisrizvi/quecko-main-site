## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
2026 is the year stablecoin regulation became enforceable, not theoretical. The US GENIUS Act is now federal law with reserve, audit, and redemption requirements; the EU's MiCA framework requires full issuer authorization; and Hong Kong has issued its first licenses under its Stablecoin Ordinance. Global market cap for stablecoins has climbed past $300B — this is now regulated financial infrastructure, and building it correctly from day one is non-negotiable.

### Body
A stablecoin isn't just a token contract — it's a reserve-backed financial instrument that must maintain its peg under stress, prove its backing to regulators and auditors, and support instant redemption without run risk. Most failures come from treating this as a smart-contract problem alone, when it's really a treasury, compliance, and engineering problem combined.

## 12. FAQs

**What backing models do you support?**
Fiat-collateralized, crypto-collateralized, and hybrid/algorithmic — matched to your regulatory and business model. Note that algorithmic stablecoins currently fall outside most regulatory frameworks.

**Do you handle reserve auditing?**
We build the attestation and reporting infrastructure; our Compliance & Legal Advisory team supports audit-readiness and coordinates with licensed auditors.

**Can holders earn yield on the stablecoin?**
Direct issuer-paid yield is prohibited under GENIUS Act and MiCA; yield products must be structured as a separate, distinctly regulated activity — something our Compliance team can help structure correctly.

**Which jurisdictions should we target first?**
This depends on your user base and banking partners — we help map the tradeoffs between US, EU, and Asian licensing pathways during the model design phase.
