## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
As stablecoin and CBDC frameworks mature globally, institutions are under growing pressure to formalize their crypto stance — not just for external regulators, but for their own risk committees. Banks are increasingly positioned as reserve custodians for stablecoin issuers, and institutions without a clear internal framework risk both missed opportunities and uncontrolled exposure when crypto activity happens informally within the organization anyway.

### Body
Institutions rarely move on crypto without internal policy sign-off first — but most internal risk and compliance teams don't yet have crypto-specific expertise, which means proposals stall in committee review for lack of a clear framework to evaluate them against.

## 12. FAQs

**Is this only for banks?**
No — this applies to any large institution (corporates, government bodies, insurers) formalizing its approach to crypto adoption.

**Does this replace external regulatory compliance work?**
No, it complements it — this is internal governance policy, distinct from external regulatory licensing or compliance consulting.

**How long does it take to get an internal framework approved?**
This depends heavily on institutional governance processes, but drafting and initial risk committee review typically takes 6–10 weeks.

**Can this framework be reused if we later launch multiple crypto products?**
Yes — the framework is designed as a durable governance foundation, so subsequent product proposals can be evaluated against it rather than requiring a fresh policy each time.
