## 12. FAQs

**Do we need a public blockchain for this?**
Usually not — most enterprise use cases run best on permissioned or consortium networks with controlled access.

**Can this integrate with our existing enterprise software?**
Yes, integration with ERP, CRM, and legacy systems is a core part of every enterprise engagement.

**How long does a typical pilot take before production rollout?**
Pilots typically run 3–6 months with a limited stakeholder group before scaling to full production.

**Who governs a consortium network once multiple companies are involved?**
Governance structure (voting rights, node operation responsibilities, dispute resolution) is designed collaboratively with all participating stakeholders during the architecture phase.
