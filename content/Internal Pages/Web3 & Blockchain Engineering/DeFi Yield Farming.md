## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
With direct yield on stablecoins now restricted under GENIUS Act and MiCA, well-designed DeFi yield farming — where returns come from real liquidity provision and trading fees, not issuer-paid interest — is becoming an even more important category for protocols offering yield-bearing products.

### Body
Yield farming is one of the fastest ways to bootstrap liquidity, but it's also one of the easiest ways to blow up a token's value if reward emissions aren't modeled carefully. Programs that pay unsustainable APYs attract mercenary capital that leaves the moment rewards drop, taking liquidity with it.

## 12. FAQs

**How do you prevent reward token inflation from collapsing value?**
Through emission modeling and stress-testing incentive structures before launch — the same economic rigor applied to all Quecko DeFi builds.

**Can this integrate with our existing AMM or lending protocol?**
Yes, yield farming is commonly layered on top of existing liquidity pools or lending markets.

**What's the difference between this and issuer-paid stablecoin yield?**
Yield here comes from real trading fees and liquidity incentives distributed to LPs — a structurally different, and currently unrestricted, model compared to issuer-paid stablecoin interest.

**How long do reward programs typically run before needing adjustment?**
Most programs need emission tuning within 3–6 months as liquidity conditions and competing incentives shift.
