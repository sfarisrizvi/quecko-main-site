## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
"Is this legal in our target market?" is the single most common objection blocking crypto product launches. The regulatory window that was "coming soon" eighteen months ago has now closed — MiCA is in full enforcement, the US GENIUS Act is federal law, and FATF's Travel Rule is enforced across roughly 70+ jurisdictions. Answering the legality question upfront de-risks every engagement and turns Quecko from a dev shop into a strategic partner.

### Body
Regulatory regimes now diverge in ways that matter operationally — the EU treats stablecoins one way, the US another, and Asia-Pacific markets are still finalizing their own frameworks. A product designed for one regime can fail compliance in another, and rules keep shifting even within a single jurisdiction as enforcement matures.

## 12. FAQs

**Do you provide formal legal opinions?**
We work alongside licensed legal counsel in relevant jurisdictions; our consulting focuses on regulatory strategy and risk mapping.

**Which markets can you advise on?**
Coverage spans major crypto hubs including the US, EU, UAE, Singapore, and Hong Kong, with jurisdiction-specific partners as needed.

**How do you determine if we're a VASP/CASP?**
FATF's test is functional, not based on labeling — we assess whether your business exercises operational control over customer transfers or assets, regardless of how the product is marketed.

**How quickly can we get a compliance risk assessment?**
A focused point-in-time assessment typically takes 2–3 weeks depending on product complexity and number of target jurisdictions.
