## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
As token classification frameworks mature (the 2026 SEC/CFTC digital asset categories being one example), tax treatment is increasingly tied to how a token is legally classified — a digital commodity, a digital security, and a stablecoin can each trigger different tax consequences for issuers and platforms. Getting ahead of this is far cheaper than restructuring after a tax authority disagrees with your assumptions.

### Body
Crypto tax treatment is still inconsistent across jurisdictions and evolving alongside broader regulatory frameworks — a token sale, staking reward, or yield distribution can be taxed completely differently depending on where the issuer and users are based, and rules keep shifting as classification frameworks solidify.

## 12. FAQs

**Do you file taxes on our behalf?**
We provide advisory and planning guidance; formal filings are handled by your licensed tax professional, which we can help identify.

**Does this cover both the project and its token holders?**
Our focus is project/platform-level tax obligations; we can point holders toward appropriate personal tax guidance.

**Does token classification affect how it's taxed?**
Often yes — whether a token is treated as a commodity, security, or currency for tax purposes frequently follows its broader legal classification, which is why we coordinate closely with our Legal Structuring team.

**How do we handle tax obligations for a global user base?**
We help map where tax nexus is likely to arise based on user distribution and platform operations, then prioritize the jurisdictions carrying the highest exposure.
