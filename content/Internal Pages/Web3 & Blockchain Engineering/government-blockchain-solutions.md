## 12. FAQs

**Is citizen data stored on a public blockchain?**
No — government deployments use permissioned networks with strict data governance and privacy controls.

**Can this integrate with existing government IT systems?**
Yes, integration with existing databases and identity systems is core to the engagement.

**How do you handle data residency requirements?**
Infrastructure is designed to meet each government's specific data residency and sovereignty rules, including on-premise or in-country hosting where required.

**What's the typical timeline from pilot to national rollout?**
Government engagements typically run 12–24 months from initial pilot to full-scale rollout, depending on procurement cycles and legal review requirements.
