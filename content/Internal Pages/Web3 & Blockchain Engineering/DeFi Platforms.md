## 3. CORE CAPABILITIES

### Headline
Comprehensive DeFi Engineering

### Grid

- **Automated Market Maker (AMM) Development:** Custom AMM design — constant product, concentrated liquidity, weighted pools, and hybrid models. We engineer pricing curves, fee structures, and impermanent loss mitigation tailored to your trading pairs and user base.

- **Lending & Borrowing Protocols:** Full lending platform architecture — collateralization engines, liquidation bots, interest rate models (variable, stable, algorithmic), and risk parameter frameworks. We build protocols that maintain solvency under extreme market conditions.

- **Yield Aggregators & Vaults:** Auto-compounding vault infrastructure, strategy routing engines, and yield optimization logic. We architect vault systems that maximize returns while managing smart contract risk across multiple underlying protocols.

- **Staking & Liquid Staking Platforms:** Validator staking infrastructure, liquid staking token (LST) engineering, and restaking protocol development. We handle reward distribution, slashing protection, and withdrawal queue mechanics.

- **Tokenomics & Economic Modeling:** Mathematical modeling of token supply dynamics, emission schedules, governance incentives, and fee distribution mechanisms. We stress-test economic models against adversarial scenarios before deployment.

- **Cross-Chain DeFi Infrastructure:** Multi-chain liquidity aggregation, cross-chain swap routing, and bridge-integrated DeFi protocols. We build products that operate seamlessly across Ethereum, L2s, Solana, and custom chains.

## 4. OUR PROCESS

### Headline
Our Engineering Approach to DeFi Development

### Step-by-Step
1. **Protocol Design & Economic Modeling:** We define the protocol's mechanism design, model tokenomics under adversarial conditions, and architect the smart contract system before development begins.
2. **Smart Contract Development & Testing:** Core protocol contracts are built with full test coverage — unit tests, integration tests, fuzz testing, and economic simulation against edge cases.
3. **Security Audit & Stress Testing:** Internal security audit, formal verification of critical invariants, economic attack simulation (flash loans, oracle manipulation, governance attacks), and coordination with external auditors.
4. **Mainnet Launch & Liquidity Strategy:** Testnet validation, mainnet deployment, liquidity bootstrapping support, frontend integration, and post-launch monitoring with automated alerting.
