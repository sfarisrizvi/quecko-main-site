## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
As tokenization expands beyond simple collectibles into ticketing, gaming economies, and loyalty programs, teams increasingly need an asset type that isn't strictly fungible or strictly unique. SFTs fill that gap — and gas-efficient batch operations make them far cheaper to issue at scale than minting thousands of individual NFTs.

### Body
Most teams default to either a fungible token or a 1-of-1 NFT because that's what they know — then discover neither fits use cases like event tickets (identical within a tier, but tracked individually) or in-game item stacks (thousands of identical swords that still need inventory tracking).

## 12. FAQs

**How is an SFT different from an NFT?**
An SFT can represent multiple identical or semi-identical units under one token ID — ideal for stackable in-game items or batch-issued tickets, unlike a strictly 1-of-1 NFT.

**Can SFTs integrate with existing NFT marketplaces?**
Yes, ERC-1155 is widely supported across major NFT marketplace infrastructure.

**Are SFTs cheaper to mint than NFTs at scale?**
Yes — batch minting under ERC-1155 significantly reduces gas costs compared to minting thousands of individual ERC-721 tokens.

**Can an SFT later be split into fully unique NFTs?**
Yes, this pattern is common for use cases like unlockable content, where a batch-issued item later reveals unique individual traits.
