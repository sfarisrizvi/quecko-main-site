## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Traditional lotteries ask players to trust a centralized operator's word on fairness. On-chain lotteries remove that trust requirement entirely — every draw, prize pool, and payout is verifiable on a public ledger, which is a genuinely differentiated pitch for a GameFi or gambling-adjacent product in a market increasingly skeptical of opaque platforms.

### Body
Randomness is the single point of failure for any lottery. If the random number generation can be predicted or manipulated by any party — including the platform operator — the entire product loses its core value proposition of fairness.

## 12. FAQs

**How is fairness guaranteed?**
Through verifiable random functions (e.g. Chainlink VRF) that make the draw process auditable on-chain.

**Is the prize pool held by a custodian?**
No — funds are held and distributed by smart contract, removing custodial risk.

**Can this support recurring jackpots that roll over between draws?**
Yes, rollover logic for unclaimed or unmatched jackpots is a standard configurable feature.

**Does regulatory status vary by jurisdiction for on-chain lotteries?**
Yes — gambling and lottery regulation varies significantly by market; we recommend pairing this build with a review from our Compliance & Legal Advisory team before launch.
