## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
FATF's Travel Rule (Recommendation 16) is now enforced across roughly 70+ jurisdictions, with thresholds ranging from a zero-value threshold in the EU to $3,000 under US rules — and FATF's March 2026 guidance extended coverage explicitly to stablecoin issuers and intermediary VASPs. A compliant KYC/AML *platform* still needs a compliant *policy* behind it that regulators actually audit against.

### Body
Written AML policy has to translate abstract regulatory principles into specific, operational rules — exact risk-scoring thresholds, exact escalation triggers, exact data fields shared under Travel Rule obligations. Generic template policies rarely survive real regulatory scrutiny.

## 12. FAQs

**Is this different from the KYC/AML platform you build?**
Yes — this is the written policy and procedure layer; the platform is the technical system that enforces it. Most clients need both.

**Can this support a licensing application?**
Yes, documented AML/CFT policy is typically a required component of VASP and MSB license applications.

**Do we need Travel Rule compliance if we only operate in one country?**
Often yes — the Travel Rule applies to any qualifying originating or beneficiary VASP transfer, including cross-border transfers your users initiate regardless of your home jurisdiction.

**How often does AML policy need to be updated?**
We recommend an annual review at minimum, with updates triggered immediately by material regulatory changes in your operating jurisdictions.
