## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
If your billing system limits your pricing model, it's limiting your growth.

### Body
Standard billing software works fine for simple "$20/month" subscriptions. But modern SaaS and enterprise services demand complexity: usage-based metering (per API call, per gigabyte), hybrid tiers, grandfathered pricing for legacy users, and complex B2B invoicing terms (Net-30). When companies outgrow basic tools, they often resort to manual spreadsheet calculations and bespoke manual invoicing, leading to revenue leakage, customer disputes, and exhausted finance teams. Building a custom billing engine requires absolute precision, flawless API integration, and unyielding security. Quecko builds automated, custom billing architectures that perfectly mirror your business model, freeing you to experiment with pricing and scale without friction.

## 12. FAQs

**Why not just use Stripe Billing?**
We love Stripe and use it constantly. However, Stripe Billing is an API; it still requires significant engineering to integrate into your app, build a customer portal, handle your specific business logic, and ingest metered usage data. For complex enterprises, Stripe is the payment rail, but the *billing engine*—the logic that dictates *what* and *when* to charge—must be custom-built to match your unique operations.

**How do you handle prorations for mid-cycle upgrades or downgrades?**
Proration math is notoriously difficult. We build robust backend logic that calculates the exact time remaining in the billing cycle, applies credits for unused time on the old plan, and calculates the new charge down to the second, ensuring seamless transitions without manual intervention.

**Can you handle B2B invoicing with Net-30 or Net-60 terms?**
Yes. We build systems that generate professional invoices, track outstanding balances, automatically send reminders, and allow clients to pay via credit card, ACH, or wire transfer, with automatic reconciliation in your database when payment is received.

**How do you ensure no usage data is lost in a metered billing setup?**
We build decoupled, event-driven architectures. The system that records usage (e.g., an API gateway recording a call) drops the event into a resilient message queue (like Kafka). A separate worker process aggregates that data and writes it to the billing database. If the billing database goes down momentarily, the queue holds the data, ensuring zero revenue leakage.
