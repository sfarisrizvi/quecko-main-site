## 12. FAQs

**Can a custom POS system process payments securely?**
Yes. We do not build payment gateways from scratch; we integrate with established, PCI-compliant payment processors (like Stripe Terminal, Adyen, or Square). The POS application handles the transaction logic and user interface, but the actual credit card data is securely tokenized and handled by the processor, keeping your systems out of PCI scope.

**How do you handle "Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store" (BOPIS)?**
BOPIS requires near-perfect inventory accuracy and solid operational workflows. We build the logic to route the online order to the correct physical store's fulfillment queue, trigger notifications for store staff to pick the item, and update the customer via SMS when the order is ready.

**What happens to the POS if the store's internet goes down?**
We build "offline-first" architecture using local databases (like SQLite or IndexedDB) on the POS device. The system continues to ring up customers and process cash (or store-and-forward credit card payments, processor permitting). When the internet returns, the local queue automatically syncs with the central cloud database to update inventory and financials.

**How do you migrate inventory data from our old, messy system?**
Data migration in retail is complex due to varied SKU formats and nested variations (size/color). We write custom ETL scripts to extract data from your old system, clean it, map it to the new, optimized database schema, and perform rigorous reconciliation checks before go-live.
