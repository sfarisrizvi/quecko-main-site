## 12. FAQs

**Isn't building a custom CRM more expensive than buying Salesforce?**
Upfront, yes. Over 3 to 5 years, almost never. When you factor in the high per-user licensing costs of enterprise platforms, plus the expensive specialized consultants required to customize and maintain them, a custom-built system that you own outright usually provides a massive long-term ROI, especially for teams over 50 users.

**How do you handle migrating our existing data?**
Data migration is treated as a critical engineering task. We write custom ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) scripts to pull your data from your current system, clean it, map it to the new optimized database schema, and verify its integrity before the new system goes live.

**Can the CRM integrate with our old, on-premise accounting software?**
Yes. Integration with legacy systems is one of our core strengths. If your legacy software has any form of API (REST, SOAP), or even if we need to implement secure database-level connections or flat-file transfers, we can build the middleware to ensure data flows securely.

**Is a custom CRM secure enough for sensitive customer data?**
We build to enterprise security standards. This includes granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), encryption of PII data both at rest and in transit, robust audit logging, and protection against common web vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10). We can also build the system to comply with specific regulatory frameworks like GDPR or HIPAA if required.
