## 4. OUR PROCESS

### Headline
Our Engineering Approach to Custom ERPs

### Step-by-Step
1. **Business Process Mapping & Architecture:** We embed with your department heads to document existing workflows, identify bottlenecks, and design a unified database schema and system architecture that eliminates data silos.
2. **Modular Development & API Integration:** We build the ERP modularly (Finance, Inventory, HR), allowing for phased rollouts. We construct robust APIs to interface with essential external systems (banks, shipping carriers).
3. **Data Migration & Integrity Testing:** Developing complex ETL pipelines to extract historical data from legacy systems, transform it to the new schema, and meticulously verify its accuracy before go-live.
4. **Phased Rollout & Change Management:** Deploying the system department by department, providing extensive training, and maintaining strict parallel processing (running the old and new systems simultaneously) until absolute stability is proven.

## 12. FAQs

**Why build a custom ERP instead of buying NetSuite or SAP?**
Off-the-shelf ERPs are designed for generic business models. If your competitive advantage relies on a unique manufacturing process or a specialized supply chain, forcing that process into a generic ERP destroys your edge. A custom ERP is built exactly for your workflows, and importantly, you own the IP and eliminate escalating per-user licensing fees.

**How do you ensure financial data is accurate and secure?**
We utilize PostgreSQL or similar enterprise-grade relational databases that enforce strict ACID (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability) compliance. Every financial movement is recorded as an immutable transaction. We also implement granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and comprehensive audit logging.

**Can the custom ERP connect to our existing machines or IoT devices?**
Yes. We have deep experience integrating software with hardware. We can build middleware that communicates with shop floor PLCs, barcode scanners, RFID readers, and IoT sensors, feeding that data directly into the ERP in real-time.

**What is the biggest risk in a custom ERP project, and how do you mitigate it?**
The biggest risk is poor adoption due to a system that doesn't actually match how the employees work. We mitigate this through exhaustive discovery phases, embedding with your team to map real workflows, and prioritizing a clean, intuitive UX that reduces data entry friction compared to their old system.
