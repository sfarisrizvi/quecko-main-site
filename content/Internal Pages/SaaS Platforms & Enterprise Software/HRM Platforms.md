## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Generic HR software turns people management into a paperwork nightmare.

### Body
Every company hires, trains, and evaluates talent differently. Yet, most organizations are forced to use generic HRM tools (like Workday or BambooHR) that require massive workarounds to fit their specific operational needs. This fragmentation creates compliance risks, data entry errors, and a terrible experience for new hires. Quecko builds custom, unified HR platforms. ## 12. FAQs

**Can the custom HRM integrate with our existing payroll provider?**
Yes. This is standard practice. Rather than building a complex, high-liability tax calculation engine from scratch, we build robust, automated API integrations with your existing payroll provider (ADP, Paychex, Gusto, etc.) to sync hours, salaries, and deductions seamlessly.

**How do you handle data privacy and compliance (like GDPR)?**
Security is engineered into the architecture from Day 1. We utilize AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS for data in transit. We implement strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) so managers only see their direct reports' data. We also build features to support GDPR compliance, such as automated data anonymization and subject access request workflows.

**Can you automate the IT provisioning process during onboarding?**
Yes. We can integrate the HRM with Identity Providers (like Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace). When an employee is marked "Hired" in the HRM, the system can automatically trigger the creation of their email account, grant access to specific software based on their role, and notify IT to ship equipment.

**What happens if we need to change our performance review structure later?**
We build performance and OKR modules to be highly configurable. Instead of hard-coding the review process, we build an administrative engine that allows your HR team to create custom forms, set new rating scales, and design new review cycles dynamically.
