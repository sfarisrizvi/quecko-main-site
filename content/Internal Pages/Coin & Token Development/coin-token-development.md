## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Every launchpad, DeFi protocol, and neobank Quecko builds needs a well-engineered token at its core. A token designed without real economic modeling almost always collapses once initial hype fades — investors increasingly scrutinize tokenomics before backing a project, not just the whitepaper's promises.

### Body
Minting a token is trivial; designing one that holds value under real market conditions is not. Supply schedules, vesting cliffs, and incentive structures that look fine on a spreadsheet often break down once early holders start selling — the difference between a token that survives its first bear market and one that doesn't comes down to the modeling done before launch.
