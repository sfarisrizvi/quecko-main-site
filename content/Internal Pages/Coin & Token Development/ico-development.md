## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Token sales remain a core fundraising mechanism for Web3 projects, but scrutiny has increased sharply as regulators worldwide tighten rules around token classification. Projects that pair technical sale infrastructure with real legal structuring stand out to serious investors — and avoid the enforcement risk that has closed down poorly-structured sales.

### Body
An ICO is part fundraising event, part regulated securities question, part smart contract system that has to handle real money without failure. Getting any one piece wrong — a contract bug, an unclear jurisdiction strategy, or a classification mistake — can sink a sale or trigger regulatory action after the fact.

## 12. FAQs

**Can you help make the sale legally compliant?**
Yes — our Compliance & Legal Advisory team handles token classification, jurisdiction structuring, and documentation review alongside the technical build.

**Do you support vesting schedules?**
Fully automated on-chain vesting and claim mechanics are standard in every ICO build.

**What happens if the sale doesn't hit its funding target?**
We build configurable soft-cap logic, including automated refund mechanics if minimum targets aren't met.

**Can this run alongside a private/seed round?**
Yes — we commonly build tiered sale structures spanning private, seed, and public rounds with different pricing and vesting per tier.
