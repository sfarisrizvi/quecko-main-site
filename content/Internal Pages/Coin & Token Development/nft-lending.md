## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
NFT holders sitting on illiquid but valuable assets — art, gaming items, real-world asset tokens — increasingly want a way to access capital without selling. NFT lending is the mechanism that turns a static collection into productive collateral, and it's a natural retention feature for any marketplace looking to keep holders engaged rather than exiting to cash out.

### Body
Unlike fungible collateral, NFTs don't have a single agreed-upon price at any moment — valuation has to account for floor price, rarity, and liquidity depth simultaneously. Getting this wrong means either over-collateralized loans nobody wants, or under-collateralized loans that leave lenders exposed.

## 12. FAQs

**How is NFT collateral valued?**
Through oracle price feeds, floor-price models, or appraisal-based methods depending on the asset class.

**Can this integrate with our existing NFT marketplace?**
Yes — NFT lending is commonly built as a companion module to an existing marketplace.

**What happens if an NFT's floor price crashes during a loan?**
Automated liquidation thresholds trigger before a loan becomes under-collateralized, with configurable grace periods and margin call mechanics.

**Does this work for illiquid or low-volume NFT collections?**
It's harder — for thin-liquidity collections, we typically recommend appraisal-based or peer-to-peer models over automated oracle pricing.
