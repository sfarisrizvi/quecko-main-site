Yes. Dedicated DevOps developers function as extension members of your internal team. They report to your CTO/Project Manager, join your daily standups, and write infrastructure files to your repositories.

All Quecko developers undergo a multi-stage vetting process: code portfolio audits, live architectural design interviews, and simulated coding reviews, ensuring they meet senior qualifications.
