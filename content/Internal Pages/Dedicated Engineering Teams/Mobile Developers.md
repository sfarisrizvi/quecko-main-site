## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Finding mobile developers who write clean UI and optimize resource usage is hard.

### Body
Developing for mobile requires unique engineering constraints: managing battery consumption, optimizing local storage memory usage, handling cellular network drop-offs, and ensuring compatibility across screen sizes. Hiring "developers" who only know how to build basic layout containers results in lagging scroll behavior, frequent app crashes, and poor user ratings on the app stores. Finding experienced mobile engineers internally takes months, during which your product window closes. Quecko provides dedicated, senior mobile developers. Our team has built neo-banking apps, social platforms, and complex Bluetooth integrations. We integrate vetted developers into your team quickly.
