## 12. FAQs

**How quickly can a designer start?**
Once layout requirements are aligned and portfolio reviews are completed, designers can typically onboard and begin working within 7 to 10 days.

**Do we manage the designers directly?**
Yes. Dedicated designers function as extension members of your internal team. They report to your Product Manager/CTO, join your daily standups, and share Figma links.

**How do you verify the skills of your designers?**
All Quecko designers undergo a multi-stage vetting process: layout portfolio audits, live UI design reviews under autolayout constraints, and communication reviews, ensuring they meet senior qualifications.

**Can we hire designers for short-term projects?**
Yes. Our standard engagement contracts start at 3 months, offering the flexibility to scale your team up or down based on your design schedule.
