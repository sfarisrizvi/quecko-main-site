## 12. FAQs

**How quickly can a Product Manager start?**
Once technical requirements are aligned and interviews are completed, PMs can typically onboard and begin working within 7 to 10 days.

**Do we manage the PMs directly?**
Yes. Dedicated PMs function as extension members of your internal team. They report to your CTO/Head of Product, join your daily standups, and write tickets to your workspaces.

**How do you verify the skills of your PMs?**
All Quecko PMs undergo a multi-stage vetting process: product roadmap audits, live agile scenario reviews under release constraints, and communication reviews, ensuring they meet senior qualifications.

**Can we hire PMs for short-term projects?**
Yes. Our standard engagement contracts start at 3 months, offering the flexibility to scale your management team up or down based on your project schedule.
