## 12. FAQs

**What is the difference between a fractional CTO and an agency developer?**
An agency developer writes code to build features. A fractional CTO provides senior leadership—designing the software architecture, planning database structures, setting security rules, and building hiring pipelines to scale your internal team.

**How many hours per week does a fractional CTO work?**
Engagements typically range from 5 to 15 hours per week, tailored to your scaling velocity. The advisor participates in key sprint planning, reviews architecture designs, and reviews candidates.

**Can a fractional CTO help us hire our internal engineering team?**
Yes. One of the main benefits is establishing hiring pipelines. We define developer requirements, review coding tests, interview senior candidates, and verify coding skills.

**How do we transition to a full-time CTO later?**
We maintain clear technical documentation of code patterns, database structures, and deployment pipelines. When you are ready, we assist in recruiting and onboarding your full-time CTO.
