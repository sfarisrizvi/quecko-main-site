## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Every exchange, neobank, and stablecoin product now operates under active regulatory enforcement — GENIUS Act, MiCA, and Hong Kong's Stablecoins Ordinance all mandate rigorous KYC/AML controls as a condition of operating legally. Platforms without production-grade compliance infrastructure are increasingly locked out of banking partnerships and exchange listings.

### Body
Compliance infrastructure has to work at two speeds simultaneously: fast enough not to create onboarding friction that kills conversion, and rigorous enough to catch genuine bad actors and satisfy regulator audits. Most teams build one and sacrifice the other.

## 12. FAQs

**Is this a policy service or a technical build?**
This is the technical platform — for policy and legal frameworks, see our Compliance & Legal Advisory services.

**Can this integrate with our existing exchange or banking platform?**
Yes, KYC/AML infrastructure is commonly built as an integrated module within a larger platform.

**What is the Travel Rule and does this support it?**
The Travel Rule requires VASPs to share originator/beneficiary information on qualifying transfers — our platforms include Travel Rule-compliant data-sharing protocols where required.

**How do you balance onboarding speed with compliance rigor?**
Through risk-tiered flows — low-risk users get streamlined onboarding, while higher-risk profiles are routed to enhanced due diligence automatically.
