## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Global trade finance still runs largely on paper-based letters of credit, bills of lading, and manual bank reconciliation — a process that can take a week or more per transaction and remains a top target for fraud. Banks and large trading houses are actively looking for digitization partners who understand both the trade workflow and the underlying distributed ledger technology.

### Body
Trade finance involves multiple banks, freight forwarders, customs authorities, and trading partners, each with their own systems and levels of digital maturity. A blockchain trade finance platform has to work across that fragmented landscape — it can't require every participant to fully replace their existing systems on day one.

## 12. FAQs

**Do all trading partners need to be on the same network?**
Multi-bank and multi-party networks are the norm — we design for interoperability across participant systems.

**How does this reduce fraud?**
Every document and transaction step is recorded immutably, making forgery and duplicate financing far harder to execute.

**Can partners join gradually rather than all at once?**
Yes — the architecture supports incremental onboarding, letting early adopters transact while others integrate over time.

**What happens to disputes over trade documents?**
The immutable audit trail simplifies dispute resolution by providing a single, verifiable record of every document version and approval step.
