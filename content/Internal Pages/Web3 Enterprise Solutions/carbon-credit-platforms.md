## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Voluntary carbon markets run through registries like Verra and Gold Standard, which together with other ICVCM-approved programs account for the vast majority of global credit volume — but fragmentation across independent registries remains the market's biggest integrity problem. Blockchain-based tracking directly addresses that gap, and European reporting rules (CSRD) are creating growing structural demand for verifiably traceable credits.

### Body
Carbon credits lose value the moment buyers doubt whether a credit was actually retired, or whether the same tonne of CO2 was claimed twice across different registries. A credible platform has to make double-counting structurally impossible, not just procedurally discouraged.

## 12. FAQs

**How does blockchain solve double-counting?**
Every credit is tokenized as a unique, traceable asset — retirement is recorded immutably, preventing the same credit from being claimed twice.

**Can this integrate with existing carbon registries?**
Yes, we design for interoperability with established verification and registry standards like Verra and Gold Standard.

**Does this replace registries like Verra?**
No — it typically complements them, mirroring and verifying registry data on-chain rather than replacing the underlying verification and issuance process.

**How does this help with CSRD or other ESG reporting requirements?**
Tokenized credits with embedded metadata make it straightforward to generate auditable, traceable reports for corporate sustainability disclosures.
