## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Insurtech remains one of the most consistently funded categories in fintech, and parametric insurance — where payouts trigger automatically on verifiable events rather than lengthy manual claims review — is the segment blockchain improves most directly. This opens doors to insurance carriers and reinsurers as enterprise clients, not just crypto-native startups.

### Body
Traditional claims processing is slow and adversarial by design — policyholders wait weeks for investigations while insurers manage fraud risk manually. A blockchain insurance product has to prove its payout logic is both fast and trustworthy to both sides of that relationship simultaneously.

## 12. FAQs

**What is parametric insurance?**
Coverage that pays out automatically when a predefined, verifiable condition is met — no lengthy claims investigation required.

**Can this integrate with traditional insurers?**
Yes, blockchain insurance products are commonly built as a complement to existing underwriting operations.

**How reliable are the oracle data feeds behind payout triggers?**
We integrate established, tamper-resistant oracle providers and design fallback mechanisms for data feed disruptions — reliability is treated as a core risk, not an afterthought.

**Does this replace the need for human claims adjusters entirely?**
For parametric products, yes for the common case; complex or disputed claims can still route to manual review as a fallback path.
