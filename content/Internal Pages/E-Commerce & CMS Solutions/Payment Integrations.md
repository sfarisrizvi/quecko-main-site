## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
A slow, unoptimized checkout page will drive away buyers at the final step.

### Body
The checkout page is the most critical part of the conversion funnel, yet it is often the most neglected technically. Bloated checkout scripts, complex forms, and lack of localized payment options lead to abandoned carts and lost sales. Furthermore, handling card inputs directly on your servers introduces massive security risks and compliance (PCI-DSS) overhead. A single leak of customer data can destroy customer trust and invite legal liabilities. Quecko builds payment checkouts with security. We integrate Stripe, Adyen, and regional processors utilizing secure tokenization models, ensuring customer data is never exposed on your servers while offering fast checkout experiences.

## 12. FAQs

**How do you protect credit card details in our application?**
We use payment SDKs that use hosted fields (like Stripe Elements). The card details are encrypted directly on input and sent directly to the gateway. Our databases only save a secure payment token, keeping your servers out of card data security scopes.

**What happens if a payment webhook fails to deliver?**
We build resilient webhook queues. If a webhook notification fails, our systems retry processing the request. We also implement backup API checks that check payment states when a user returns to the success page.

**Can we split payouts to multiple vendors automatically?**
Yes. We integrate multi-party split engines (like Stripe Connect). When a customer checks out, the payment engine processes the transaction, deducts your platform fee, and routes the split payouts to vendors' accounts automatically.

**Do you support local payment methods for Europe or Asia?**
Yes. We configure gateways to check user locations and dynamically display localized payment options—Klarna, Bancontact, iDEAL, or Giropay—increasing conversion rates for international traffic.
