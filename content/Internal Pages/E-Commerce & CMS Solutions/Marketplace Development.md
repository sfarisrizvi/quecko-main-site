## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Building a marketplace is not just building a store. It's building an entire economy.

### Body
A single-merchant store is straightforward: one seller, one catalog, one checkout. A multi-vendor marketplace is exponentially more complex. It requires robust data boundaries where vendor inventories are securely isolated, yet checkout remains single and unified for the customer. It requires onboarding verification pipelines (KYC), automatic payment split engines, shipping calculation rules across multiple warehouses, and automated catalog moderation to prevent illegal or low-quality listings. Attempting to build this with generic plugins leads to performance bottlenecks and transactional discrepancies. Quecko builds custom marketplace platforms from the ground up, utilizing modern modular architectures, secure payment splits, and scalable database backends.

## 12. FAQs

**How do payments get split between the marketplace and vendors?**
We integrate payment routing engines (like Stripe Connect). When a customer checks out, the engine processes the transaction, routes the purchase amount to the vendor's account, and deposits the platform commission into your platform account automatically.

**How do we verify vendor identities to prevent fraud?**
We integrate with automated KYC (Know Your Customer) identity systems. During vendor onboarding, vendors submit verification documents which are securely processed by the payment provider (e.g., Stripe Custom Connect) before they can receive payouts.

**Can a user buy from two different vendors in one order?**
Yes. We build unified cart and checkout architectures. The checkout system calculates taxes and shipping parameters for each vendor location independently, processes the single charge, and routes the individual line-item orders to the respective vendors' dashboards.

**Where is vendor inventory stored?**
Inventory details are tracked in isolated tables in the relational database. If a vendor uses external inventory managers, we build custom API integrations to sync stock quantities to their vendor profiles automatically.
