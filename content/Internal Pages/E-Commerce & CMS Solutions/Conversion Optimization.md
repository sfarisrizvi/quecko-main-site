## 12. FAQs

**How much traffic do we need to run A/B tests?**
To get statistically valid results from A/B testing, we recommend having at least 1,000 conversions (sales) per month. For lower-traffic stores, we focus on speed optimizations and fixing obvious user experience bugs first.

**How does site speed affect conversion rates?**
Site speed is directly linked to conversions. Research shows that e-commerce sites loading in under 2 seconds convert at double the rate of sites that take 4 seconds or longer. Lower load speeds reduce bounce rates.

**Will running A/B tests slow down our site?**
If implemented poorly, yes. Some testing tools load heavy scripts that cause page layout flicker. We prevent this by writing lightweight testing code and loading scripts asynchronously to preserve site speed.

**How do we identify where users are leaving our store?**
We use funnel analysis in Mixpanel/GA4 to track user journeys. We then watch screen recordings and heatmaps (Hotjar) on high-drop-off pages to understand why users are leaving.
