## 12. FAQs

**What is Checkout Extensibility and why is it required?**
Shopify has deprecated legacy `checkout.liquid` layouts for security and speed. Checkout Extensibility is the new API-driven framework that allows developers to build app-like blocks (upsells, custom fields, checks) directly within the secure Shopify checkout flow.

**How does Shopify Plus handle B2B wholesale?**
Shopify Plus includes native B2B capabilities, allowing you to run your D2C and wholesale operations from a single store. It supports custom price lists, payment term options (like Net-30), bulk order forms, and customer profile management.

**Can we build a headless store using Shopify Hydrogen?**
Yes. Hydrogen is Shopify's official headless commerce framework built on React and Remix. We build custom frontends in Hydrogen and deploy them to Shopify's Oxygen hosting platform for ultra-fast, global delivery.

**How do you sync inventory between Shopify Plus and our ERP?**
We build event-driven middleware. When stock allocations change in your ERP (NetSuite, SAP), the system triggers a webhook to our middleware API, which updates Shopify Plus stock quantities in real-time.
