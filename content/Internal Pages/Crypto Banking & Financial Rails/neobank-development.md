## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Digital-only banking has moved from novelty to default — neobanks now serve hundreds of millions of users globally, and the fastest-growing ones are the ones layering in crypto rails to serve underbanked and crypto-native users at once. For investors, a neobank is a recurring-revenue platform (interchange, FX spread, subscription tiers), not a one-off build.

### Body
Launching a neobank means shipping a regulated financial product, not just an app. Founders have to get the core banking ledger, card issuance, KYC onboarding, and crypto integration all working together — while satisfying banking partners and regulators who won't tolerate shortcuts on custody or compliance.

**Can this be white-labeled for our brand?**

We architect for compliance and partner with licensed banking-as-a-service providers; our Compliance & Legal Advisory team supports the regulatory side.

Yes — the architecture is modular, so crypto wallets and conversion can be added post-launch without rebuilding the core.
