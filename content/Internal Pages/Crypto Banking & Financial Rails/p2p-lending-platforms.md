## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Retail investors are actively searching for transparent, on-chain yield alternatives now that stablecoin-paid yield is restricted under GENIUS Act and MiCA. A well-structured P2P lending product — where yield comes from real borrower demand, not issuer-paid interest — sits cleanly outside that regulatory gray zone while still delivering the return profile users want.

### Body
Lending platforms live or die on risk management. Get collateral ratios, liquidation triggers, or credit scoring wrong, and a single market swing can cascade into platform-wide insolvency. The engineering has to be as rigorous as the product design.

## 12. FAQs

**Can this support both crypto and real-world asset collateral?**
Yes — collateral logic can be built for crypto assets, tokenized RWAs, or a hybrid model.

**How is default risk managed?**
Automated liquidation thresholds and risk-based rate models are built into the core lending logic, stress-tested before launch.

**Is this peer-to-peer matching or a pooled model?**
We build either — pooled liquidity is simpler to bootstrap, while direct P2P matching offers more borrower/lender customization.

**How does this differ from a DeFi lending protocol?**
P2P lending platforms typically include KYC-gated onboarding and centralized risk controls suited to regulated markets, whereas DeFi lending protocols are usually permissionless.
