## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Over 130 countries, representing nearly the entire global economy, are now exploring or piloting a CBDC. The UAE's Digital Dirham is expanding into peer-to-peer and cross-border use cases through 2026 via the mBridge platform; India's e-Rupee circulation has grown sharply; and Hong Kong, South Korea, and the EU are all advancing active pilots. Central banks are moving from research to real deployment, and technical delivery partners are in short supply.

### Body
A CBDC isn't just a digital token — it's national payment infrastructure that must handle offline transactions, tiered privacy for citizens, interoperability with existing banking rails, and security standards far beyond a typical fintech product. Central banks need partners who understand both the monetary policy context and the underlying distributed ledger engineering.

## 12. FAQs

**Do you support both retail and wholesale CBDC models?**
Yes — architecture is scoped to the central bank's specific use case and monetary policy requirements.

**How is privacy balanced with regulatory oversight?**
We design tiered-privacy models that satisfy AML/CFT requirements while protecting user-level transaction data, similar to the approach used in leading retail CBDC pilots.

**Can a CBDC settle cross-border with other countries' CBDCs?**
Yes, through interoperability platforms like mBridge — we design for this compatibility from the architecture phase.

**How long does a CBDC pilot typically take to launch?**
Most pilots run 6–18 months from architecture workshop to limited public testing, depending on scope and regulatory approval timelines.
