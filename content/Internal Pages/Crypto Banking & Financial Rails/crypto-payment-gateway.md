## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Stablecoins have crossed into mainstream payment infrastructure, with regulated frameworks like the US GENIUS Act and EU MiCA now treating them as payment instruments rather than speculative assets. That regulatory clarity is exactly what merchants and platforms have been waiting for before integrating crypto checkout.

### Body
Merchants want the upside of crypto payments — lower fees, faster settlement, global reach — without taking on volatility risk or building blockchain infrastructure themselves. A gateway has to hide that complexity entirely behind a simple checkout experience.
