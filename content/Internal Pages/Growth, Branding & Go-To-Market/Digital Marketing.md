## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Wasting budget on unoptimized ads is common. You need conversion-focused campaigns.

### Body
Running digital marketing campaigns without verifying conversion attribution tracking results in wasted advertising budgets. Many marketing agencies focus on impressions or clicks rather than leads and revenue. Furthermore, setting up uncoordinated campaign structures across platforms leads to database inconsistencies and poor targeting. To scale acquisition successfully, you need tracking setups, landing pages that load instantly, and campaign parameters that align with actual conversions. Quecko builds digital growth strategies using code and data—engineering event tracking, optimizing landing pages, and tuning campaign budgets to hit revenue metrics.

## 12. FAQs

**How do you guarantee conversion pixels are tracking correctly?**
We use server-side tracking (Conversions API) alongside browser pixels, verifying data schemas to prevent duplicate events and track transactions accurately.

**Why build custom landing pages instead of using Unbounce or Instapage?**
Landing page builders add heavy JavaScript libraries that slow page loading down. A custom Next.js landing page loads in milliseconds, lowering bounce rates from ad clicks.

**What is the minimum ad spend required?**
We recommend starting with an ad budget of at least $3,000/month to ensure we gather enough data to optimize bidding and creatives.

**What is the setup time for a complete marketing campaign?**
Timelines typically range from 4 to 6 weeks based on landing page designs, ad creative asset pipelines, and database integrations required.
