## 12. FAQs

**How do you prevent spam bots from joining our community?**
We implement multi-step verification gates (like captcha bots or phone validations) that users must complete before accessing channel directories, keeping spam accounts out.

**Can you setup token-gated channels on Discord?**
Yes. We integrate token-gating tools (like Guild.xyz) to check user wallets dynamically. If the user owns the required token or NFT, the system grants access to exclusive channels automatically.

**What is the role of Zealy in community growth?**
Zealy allows us to gamify community engagement. Users complete tasks (like sharing announcements or joining events) to earn experience points (XP) and level up on a public leaderboard.

**What is the setup time for a complete Discord server launch?**
Timelines typically range from 4 to 6 weeks based on category complexity, bot configurations, and quest campaign requirements.
