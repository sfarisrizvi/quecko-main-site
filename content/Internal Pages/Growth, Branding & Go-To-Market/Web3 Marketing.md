Yes. Our technical writers work alongside smart contract engineers to write clear, technically accurate explainers and developer guides.

**What is the role of Galxe or Zealy in community building?**
These platforms allow us to gamify community engagement. Users complete tasks (like sharing posts or linking wallets) to earn quest points and level up in the community.
