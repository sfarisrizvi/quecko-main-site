### Headline

Many influencer campaigns fail because brands hire accounts with inflated follower counts and low engagement. Evaluating influencer reach manually is time-consuming, while uncoordinated campaigns lead to lack of tracking data and wasted marketing spend. To run influencer campaigns successfully, you need verified metrics, structured campaign tracking parameters (UTMs), and performance-based contracts. Quecko builds influencer strategies using data—maintaining lists of verified KOLs, auditing engagement metrics, and building dashboard ledgers to track conversion return on spend (ROAS).

**Can we negotiate performance-based payouts?**
Yes. We write contracts that include base fees alongside bonuses linked to conversion metrics, optimizing campaign spend.

Timelines typically range from 4 to 6 weeks based on negotiator agreements, storyboard reviews, and posting schedules.
