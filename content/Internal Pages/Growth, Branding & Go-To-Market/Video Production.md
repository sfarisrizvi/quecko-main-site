## 12. FAQs

**Can you edit videos from raw app recordings?**
Yes. You send us raw recordings of your software or app interface, and we edit them, zoom in on key features, and add custom motion callouts.

**How long does it take to build a 60-second product video?**
Timelines typically range from 4 to 6 weeks based on storyboard iterations, voiceover recordings, and motion graphics complexity.

**Do you provide voiceover recordings?**
Yes. We coordinate with professional voiceover talent to record scripts in various languages and accents, integrating them into the final mix.

**What formats do you deliver for social ads?**
We deliver optimized video files in widescreen (16:9) for YouTube, square (1:1) for feeds, and vertical (9:16) for stories/reels.
