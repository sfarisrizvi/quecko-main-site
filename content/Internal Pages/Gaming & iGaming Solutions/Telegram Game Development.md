## 12. FAQs

**How long does it take to build a Telegram game?**
Simple tap-to-earn games can be built in 4–6 weeks. More complex games with multiplayer, token economies, and social systems typically take 8–12 weeks. We scope timelines based on your specific game design.

**Can Telegram games integrate with crypto/tokens?**
Yes. We integrate TON wallets, token rewards, NFT drops, and on-chain achievements. We can also bridge to EVM chains for projects with existing token ecosystems on Ethereum, BSC, or Polygon.

**How do you prevent bots from farming rewards?**
We implement multi-layered anti-cheat — server-side action validation, rate limiting, behavioral analysis, device fingerprinting, and proof-of-humanity checks. Every reward-generating action is verified server-side.

**What monetization models work for Telegram games?**
In-game purchases (cosmetics, boosts), token economies (play-to-earn), ad-based monetization (rewarded video), premium subscriptions, and sponsored content. We recommend models based on your audience and game genre.
