## 12. FAQs

**Why do we need custom indexing instead of just using RPC nodes?**
Directly querying RPC nodes for complex game state (like leaderboards or inventory filtering) is incredibly slow and expensive at scale. Custom indexers (like a Subgraph) listen to blockchain events, process them, and store them in an optimized database (like PostgreSQL), allowing your game to query the data instantly via GraphQL or REST.

**Should we build on an existing L2 or launch our own AppChain?**
It depends on your game's required throughput, desired level of customization, and economic model. An existing L2 (like Arbitrum or Polygon) offers easier composability and existing liquidity. An AppChain (like an Avalanche Subnet) offers dedicated blockspace, custom gas tokens, and ultimate control, but requires more infrastructure overhead. We help you make the right architectural choice.

**How do you handle smart contract upgradability?**
Game logic often needs tweaking post-launch. We implement secure upgradability patterns, such as the Diamond Pattern (EIP-2535) or Transparent Proxies, controlled by multi-sig wallets or DAO governance, ensuring the game can evolve without compromising trust.

**Do you assist with security audits?**
Yes. While we conduct rigorous internal security reviews and automated testing, we strongly mandate external audits for all GameFi infrastructure. We have established relationships with top-tier auditing firms and will coordinate the audit process, address findings, and ensure the code is secure before mainnet deployment.
