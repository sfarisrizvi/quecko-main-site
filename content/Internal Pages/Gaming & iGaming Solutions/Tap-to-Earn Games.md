## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Anyone can build a button that gives points. Very few can build an economy that doesn't collapse.

### Body
Tap-to-earn (T2E) games are simple on the surface, but the math behind them is brutal. If the progression curve is too fast, players max out and leave. If the token emission is too high, the economy hyper-inflates and the token crashes. And if you launch without server-side validation, bot farms will drain your rewards pool in 48 hours. Most teams ship a flashy frontend with no economic modeling and no anti-cheat, leading to a dead game and an angry community. Quecko builds T2E games from the math up — engineering addictive progression curves, sustainable token sinks, and military-grade bot protection that ensures real players get the rewards.

## 12. FAQs

**How do you stop auto-clickers?**
We don't trust the client. Every tap is sent to the server, which validates the timing, frequency, and pattern against human capability limits and energy constraints. We also use device fingerprinting and behavioral analysis to flag and shadowban bot accounts.

**Can the game run inside Telegram?**
Yes, that's our specialty. We build Tap-to-Earn games as Telegram Web Apps (TWAs), providing a native-app-like experience directly inside the messenger, leveraging Telegram's social graph for viral growth.

**How do we make sure the game remains engaging after a week?**
Through deep progression systems (upgrades, leagues, seasons), social competition (squads, leaderboards), and passive income mechanics (idle earning). The goal is to transition the player from active tapping to strategic upgrading and social interaction.

**How does token integration work?**
Players earn off-chain points in the game, which can later be converted to on-chain tokens via an integrated wallet connection (like TON Space or MetaMask) during a TGE (Token Generation Event) or periodic airdrops.
