## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Most P2E games are just Ponzi schemes disguised as gameplay. Yours needs to be a real game.

### Body
The first generation of Play-to-Earn proved the model, but destroyed the economies. Games relied entirely on new player influx to pay old players, leading to inevitable hyper-inflation and death spirals. Building a modern P2E platform requires more than slapping a token on a simple game loop. It demands rigorous economic modeling (sinks vs. faucets), true utility for in-game assets, gas-less transactions to remove friction, and gameplay that is actually fun enough to retain players who aren't just there to extract value. Quecko builds P2E platforms with balanced mechanics, secure smart contracts, and seamless UX that bridges the gap between Web2 gamers and Web3 ownership.

## 3. CORE CAPABILITIES

### Headline
Comprehensive P2E Platform Engineering

### Grid

- **Dual-Token Economy Design:** Engineering sustainable economic models utilizing a governance token (fixed supply) and an in-game utility token (elastic supply), balanced with carefully designed sinks, burning mechanisms, and reward pools.

- **NFT Asset Integration & Minting:** Developing dynamic, upgradable in-game assets (characters, land, weapons) as NFTs (ERC-721/1155). We build the infrastructure for minting, breeding/crafting, and on-chain metadata management.

- **In-Game Marketplaces:** Secure, high-performance secondary markets where players can trade, rent, or auction their assets. We implement custom royalty structures, gas-optimized trading contracts, and fiat on-ramps.

- **Account Abstraction & Gasless UX:** Removing Web3 friction for mainstream gamers. We implement social logins, session keys, and sponsored transactions (gasless relayed transactions) so players can play without managing seed phrases or gas fees.

- **Guild & Scholarship Infrastructure:** Built-in systems for gaming guilds and managers to securely lend assets to scholars (players) with automated, trustless revenue splitting enforced via smart contracts.

- **Cross-Chain & Layer 2 Scaling:** Deploying high-throughput game logic on specific L2s (Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Immutable X) or custom subnets/app-chains to ensure fast, cheap transactions while maintaining mainnet security.

## 12. FAQs

**Do you build the actual game or just the Web3 platform?**
We primarily focus on the Web3 platform infrastructure—smart contracts, tokenomics logic, in-game marketplaces, account abstraction, and backend integration. We typically partner with your Unity/Unreal game development team to integrate this infrastructure into the game client.

**How do we prevent our P2E token from crashing?**
Through sustainable economic design. We help you implement multiple strong token sinks (crafting, upgrading, tournament entry fees, cosmetics) and limit faucets (emissions). We also strongly advocate for a dual-token model to separate governance from in-game utility.

**Can we launch a P2E game without forcing players to buy crypto first?**
Yes. By utilizing Account Abstraction (ERC-4337) and fiat on-ramps, players can sign up with an email/social account, and you can sponsor their gas fees. They can buy in-game assets using a credit card, completely abstracting the blockchain complexity.

**Which blockchain is best for a P2E game?**
Ethereum mainnet is too expensive. We recommend gaming-optimized Layer 2s like Immutable X, Arbitrum, or Polygon, or high-throughput L1s like Solana or Avalanche (Custom Subnets), depending on your specific throughput and ecosystem requirements.
