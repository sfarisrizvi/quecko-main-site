## 12. FAQs

**Do you build the game client (Unity/Unreal) or just the blockchain backend?**
We specialize in the Web3 infrastructure—smart contracts, indexing, backend services, and SDK integration. While we have frontend capabilities for web-based games, for 3D/AAA titles, we typically act as the Web3 engineering partner working alongside your dedicated Unity/Unreal studio.

**What is a "Fully On-Chain Game" (FOCG)?**
In a FOCG, the entire game state and logic (rules, physics, player positions) live on the blockchain, not just the assets. This requires specialized frameworks like MUD or Dojo and allows for permissionless modding and ultimate interoperability, though it's typically suited for specific genres like strategy games.

**How do you solve the issue of players having to pay gas fees?**
We use Account Abstraction (ERC-4337) and Paymasters. This allows the game developer to sponsor transaction fees for the players, meaning players can perform on-chain actions without needing to hold the native gas token (like ETH or MATIC) in their wallets.

**Can assets be upgraded or changed dynamically?**
Yes. We implement advanced token standards (like ERC-6551 or dynamic metadata handling) that allow NFTs to own other NFTs (e.g., a character equipping a sword) or change their visual representation and stats based on in-game actions.
