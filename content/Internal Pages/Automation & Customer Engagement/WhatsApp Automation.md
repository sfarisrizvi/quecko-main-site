## 12. FAQs

**What is the difference between WhatsApp Business App and the API?**
The Business App is for small shops using a single phone. The WhatsApp Business API is for scaling companies, allowing you to connect custom code, automate chatbots, support multiple agents on a shared inbox, and send broadcasts.

**Can we send marketing broadcasts to users?**
Yes. By using pre-approved Meta message templates, you can send broadcasts to opt-in contact lists. We build opt-out logic into your campaigns to protect your sender rating.

**How do we prevent our phone number from getting banned?**
We build official, API-compliant integrations. Unofficial scripting tools that scrape WhatsApp Web violate terms of service and lead to phone number bans. We guide your templates through Meta's approval process to ensure safety.

**Can the bot route complex queries to a live agent?**
Yes. We build intelligent routing workflows. When a user asks a complex question or requests human assistance, the bot assigns the conversation to your support dashboard, notifying your live agents.
