## 12. FAQs

**How does automated lead enrichment work?**
When a user submits an email, the system sends it to an enrichment API (like Clearbit). The API matches the domain and returns company size, industry, and funding details to your CRM automatically.

**Can we route leads dynamically based on territory?**
Yes. We build custom routing rules. When a lead is captured, the engine checks their location details and routes the opportunity to the correct sales rep's queue.

**Can the system validate phone numbers on input?**
Yes. We integrate phone verification APIs (like Twilio Lookup). The form checks if the phone number is active and formatted correctly before allowing submission.

**What is the setup time for a complete lead gen project?**
Timelines typically range from 6 to 8 weeks based on form complexity, calculator logic, and database integrations required.
