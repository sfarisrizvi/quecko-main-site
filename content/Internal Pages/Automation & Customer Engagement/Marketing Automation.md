## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Generic blasts annoy customers. Uncoordinated campaigns waste marketing spend.

## 12. FAQs

**Can the marketing system sync directly with our web database?**
Yes. We build event-tracking pipelines that capture user actions in real-time, syncing details directly with your marketing tools or internal databases.

**How do we prevent emails from going to the spam folder?**
We configure domain keys (DKIM, SPF, DMARC), implement double opt-in verification, setup unsubscribe trackers, and monitor bounce rates to protect your domain rating.

**Can we send SMS campaigns dynamically based on user behavior?**
Yes. We integrate SMS APIs (like Twilio) to trigger text messages dynamically—such as sending a checkout discount code 30 minutes after an abandoned cart.

**What is the setup time for a complete automation project?**
Timelines typically range from 6 to 8 weeks based on the number of marketing channels consolidated and database integrations required.
