## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Rigid decision-tree bots frustrate customers. Hallucinating AI bots create liability.

### Body
Standard decision-tree chatbots force users through rigid menus and fail to resolve basic variations of questions. Conversely, deploying generative AI bots without strict safety guardrails and retrieval pipelines results in "hallucinations"—where the bot provides incorrect information, promises discount codes, or exposes internal data. To deploy an AI chatbot successfully, you need context-aware retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), secure API connections to check account states, and structured guardrails. Quecko builds bespoke AI chatbots, connecting secure local databases and APIs to model logic so the chatbot resolves queries accurately and securely.

## 12. FAQs

**How do you prevent the AI chatbot from hallucinating?**
We use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The bot is restricted to querying your company's vector knowledge base. We also set up semantic guardrails that block the LLM from answering questions outside your document context.

**Can the chatbot check order statuses in real-time?**
Yes. We connect the chatbot to your APIs. When a customer asks "Where is my order?", the bot prompts for validation (like phone/email), fetches the status from your database, and answers dynamically.

**Do you support deploying the chatbot on WhatsApp?**
Yes. We build and deploy the chatbot logic across web chat widgets, official WhatsApp Business API, and Facebook Messenger, maintaining a shared backend.

**What is the training cost comparison?**
Using fine-tuned open-source models reduces LLM API costs at scale. We help you choose the right balance of model size and API requirements based on query volume.
