## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Licensing is no longer optional paperwork — it's an operational gate. Non-compliant exchanges and stablecoin issuers are actively losing banking relationships and market access as regulators enforce authorization deadlines (MiCA's July 2026 cutoff, Hong Kong's new Stablecoins Ordinance licenses). Guiding clients through licensing rather than leaving them to figure it out post-build closes the loop between Quecko's technical and advisory services.

### Body
Licensing applications require extensive documentation — governance structures, AML policies, capital adequacy proof, technical architecture disclosures — and the requirements differ meaningfully between jurisdictions. Missing a single required disclosure can delay approval by months.

## 12. FAQs

**Can you obtain the license on our behalf?**
We prepare and guide the process; formal submission typically requires a locally licensed legal representative, which we help coordinate.

**How long does licensing typically take?**
Timelines vary significantly by jurisdiction — from a few months to over a year for full VASP licenses.

**What happens if our application is rejected or delayed?**
We support remediation — addressing regulator queries, resubmitting missing documentation, and adjusting the application based on feedback.

**Do we need a license in every country our users are based in?**
Not necessarily — licensing requirements depend on where you actively solicit or serve customers, which we help assess during jurisdiction selection.
