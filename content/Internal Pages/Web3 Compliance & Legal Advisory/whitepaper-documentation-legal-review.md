## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Under MiCA, crypto-asset whitepapers are now a formal regulatory filing requirement for many token issuers in the EU, not just a marketing document — and regulators elsewhere increasingly treat whitepaper language as evidence in classification disputes. A promise made casually in a whitepaper ("guaranteed returns," "profit-sharing roadmap") can retroactively push a token into securities territory.

### Body
Whitepapers are usually written by founders or marketing teams optimizing for investor excitement, not legal defensibility — exactly the incentive structure that produces language regulators later flag as problematic.

## 12. FAQs

**Do you rewrite the whitepaper for us?**

**Is a whitepaper legally required for our token?**
Under MiCA, many token issuers in the EU must file a compliant crypto-asset whitepaper before offering tokens to the public — we help determine if this applies to you.
