## 12. FAQs

**Is AI governance legally required?**
Increasingly, yes. The EU AI Act creates enforceable requirements for high-risk AI systems. GDPR includes rights related to automated decision-making. Sector-specific regulations (finance, healthcare) add additional requirements. Governance is becoming a legal baseline, not optional best practice.

**Can you add governance to AI systems already in production?**
Yes. We retrofit governance controls — bias testing, explainability, audit trails — onto existing AI systems without requiring a full rebuild.

**What's the difference between AI governance and AI ethics?**
AI ethics is a set of principles. AI governance is the technical and organizational infrastructure that enforces those principles — bias testing, audit trails, explainability, access controls, and regulatory compliance.

**How do you handle bias testing?**
We test for demographic, selection, and measurement bias across protected attributes. We use statistical fairness metrics, run adversarial testing, and implement mitigation techniques at the data, model, and output level.
