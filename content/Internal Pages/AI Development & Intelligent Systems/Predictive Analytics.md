## 4. OUR PROCESS

### Headline
Our Engineering Approach to Predictive Analytics

### Step-by-Step
1. **Data Audit & Problem Framing:** We assess your data sources, quality, and coverage. We define the prediction target, success metrics, and how predictions will integrate into business decisions.
2. **Data Pipeline & Feature Engineering:** Clean, transform, and enrich your data. Build feature engineering pipelines that extract predictive signals from raw data — the step that makes or breaks model performance.
3. **Model Development & Backtesting:** Train, evaluate, and compare multiple model architectures. Rigorous backtesting against historical data with cross-validation, holdout testing, and business metric evaluation.
4. **Deployment & Decision Integration:** Embed predictions into dashboards, APIs, or automated workflows. Deploy with monitoring for model drift, accuracy degradation, and retraining triggers.

## 12. FAQs

**What types of predictions can you build?**
Demand forecasting, churn prediction, revenue forecasting, risk scoring, fraud detection, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, lead scoring, and any business outcome that can be modeled from historical data.

**What data do we need to get started?**
At minimum, historical data relevant to the prediction target — transaction records, customer behavior logs, sensor data, or operational metrics. We assess data quality and coverage during the discovery phase and recommend enrichment strategies if gaps exist.

**How accurate are predictive models?**
Accuracy depends on data quality, volume, and the prediction complexity. We define accuracy benchmarks during problem framing and rigorously test against them. We won't deploy a model that doesn't meet agreed-upon thresholds.

**Do models need to be retrained over time?**
Yes. Business conditions change, and models drift. We build automated retraining pipelines and monitoring systems that detect when model performance degrades and trigger retraining with fresh data.
