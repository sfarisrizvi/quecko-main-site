## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Rule-based automation breaks the moment reality gets messy. AI automation adapts.

### Body
Traditional automation (RPA, macros, if/then scripts) works until the input changes format, the process varies, or an exception doesn't fit the rules. Then it breaks, generates errors, and creates more work than it saved. Most businesses have processes that are too complex for rigid scripts but too repetitive for human labor — document processing with variable formats, decision-making with contextual nuance, and workflows that need to handle exceptions gracefully. Quecko builds AI automation that understands context, processes unstructured inputs, makes intelligent decisions, and handles edge cases — turning your most time-consuming manual processes into reliable, scalable systems.

## 12. FAQs

**How is AI automation different from RPA?**
RPA follows rigid, pre-programmed rules and breaks when inputs vary. AI automation understands unstructured data, adapts to variations, makes contextual decisions, and handles exceptions intelligently. We build AI automation for the complex workflows that RPA can't handle.

**What processes can be automated with AI?**
Document processing, data entry, email classification, approval routing, data validation, report generation, compliance checks, and any high-volume, repetitive workflow where inputs are variable or decisions require context.

**How do you handle cases where the AI isn't confident?**
We build human-in-the-loop escalation paths — when the AI's confidence falls below a defined threshold, the task is routed to a human reviewer with full context. Over time, these escalations feed back into model retraining.

**How do you measure automation success?**
We track manual effort eliminated (hours saved), automation accuracy, throughput (tasks processed per unit time), exception rates, and cost savings — with dashboards that make ROI visible to stakeholders.
