## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Most AI strategies fail because they start with technology instead of business outcomes.

### Body
Organizations hear about AI and jump to "let's build a chatbot" or "we need an LLM" without first understanding which business problems AI can meaningfully solve, whether their data supports it, or how to measure success. The result: expensive proof-of-concepts that never reach production, AI tools that employees ignore, and boards questioning whether the investment was worth it. What's needed is a strategy that starts with business impact — identifying the highest-ROI use cases, assessing technical feasibility, and creating a roadmap that your engineering team (or ours) can actually execute. Quecko provides AI strategy that bridges the gap between executive ambition and engineering reality.

## 12. FAQs

**How long does an AI strategy engagement take?**
Typically 60–90 days for a comprehensive strategy covering readiness assessment, use case prioritization, and roadmap development. Focused workshops on specific topics can be completed in 1–2 weeks.

**Do we need to use Quecko for implementation after the strategy?**
No. Our strategies are designed to be executable by any competent engineering team — including your internal team, another vendor, or Quecko. However, we offer a seamless transition from strategy to execution for organizations that want continuity.

**How do you prioritize which AI use cases to tackle first?**
We score opportunities across four dimensions: business impact (revenue, cost savings, strategic value), technical feasibility, data readiness, and implementation complexity. We prioritize initiatives with the highest impact-to-effort ratio.

**What if our data isn't ready for AI?**
That's a common finding — and it's valuable to know before investing in models. Our strategy includes a data readiness assessment and recommendations for the data infrastructure improvements needed to support your AI initiatives.
