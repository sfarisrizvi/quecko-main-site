## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Users don't want to talk to a decision tree. They want a conversation that actually solves their problem.

### Body
Most chatbots are glorified FAQ pages with a text input. They can't handle multi-turn context, misunderstand intent, give generic responses, and frustrate users into calling support anyway. Building a conversational AI that genuinely understands user intent, maintains context across turns, retrieves accurate information from your knowledge base, and knows when to escalate to a human — requires NLU engineering, dialogue management, and retrieval-augmented generation that most teams don't have in-house. Quecko builds conversational systems that resolve real issues, reduce support costs, and leave users feeling like they had a useful interaction.

## 3. CORE CAPABILITIES

### Headline
Comprehensive Conversational AI Engineering

### Grid

- **Multi-Turn Dialogue Systems:** Context-aware dialogue engines that track conversation state across multiple turns, handle topic switching, and maintain coherent responses throughout complex interactions — not just single-question FAQ bots.

- **Intent Recognition & Entity Extraction:** NLU pipelines that accurately classify user intent and extract structured entities (dates, product names, order numbers, locations) from natural language — trained on your domain-specific vocabulary.

- **Knowledge-Grounded Responses (RAG):** Retrieval-augmented generation systems that ground AI responses in your knowledge base, documentation, and product data — delivering accurate, citation-backed answers instead of hallucinated responses.

- **Human Handoff & Escalation Logic:** Intelligent escalation systems that detect when the AI can't resolve an issue, seamlessly transfer context to a human agent, and route based on topic, urgency, and customer tier.

- **Voice AI & Speech Interfaces:** Voice-based conversational AI with speech-to-text, natural language understanding, and text-to-speech — deployed as IVR replacements, voice assistants, or phone-based support systems.

- **Omnichannel Deployment:** Deploy your conversational AI across web chat, mobile apps, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, email, and voice — with unified conversation history and consistent behavior across channels.

1. **Dialogue Design & NLU Architecture:** We map conversation flows, define intents and entities, design fallback strategies, and select the NLU/LLM backbone. We analyze your existing support data to identify high-volume intents.

## 12. FAQs

**What's the difference between a chatbot and conversational AI?**
A chatbot typically follows scripted decision trees. Conversational AI uses natural language understanding, multi-turn context tracking, and knowledge retrieval to have genuine, adaptive conversations. We build the latter.

**Can you integrate with our existing CRM and helpdesk?**
Yes. We integrate with Zendesk, Intercom, Salesforce, HubSpot, Freshdesk, and custom support platforms — syncing conversation context, ticket creation, and customer data.

**How do you handle questions the AI can't answer?**
We build intelligent escalation systems that detect uncertainty, transfer full conversation context to human agents, and route based on topic, urgency, and customer tier — ensuring seamless handoff without users repeating themselves.

**What channels can the conversational AI be deployed on?**
Web chat, mobile apps, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, email, voice (phone/IVR), and custom integrations — all sharing unified conversation history and consistent behavior.
