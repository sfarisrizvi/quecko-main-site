## 4. OUR PROCESS

### Headline
Our Engineering Approach to Custom AI Development

### Step-by-Step
1. **Problem Definition & Data Assessment:** We define the AI problem precisely, assess your available data, identify data gaps, and determine the right ML approach (fine-tuning, RAG, custom training, or hybrid).
2. **Model Development & Training:** Data pipeline construction, model selection, fine-tuning, evaluation benchmarking, and iterative optimization until the model meets accuracy and latency requirements.
3. **Application Development & Integration:** Full-stack application development — backend APIs, frontend interfaces, database integrations, and deployment infrastructure — wrapping the model in a usable product.
4. **Deployment, Monitoring & Optimization:** Production deployment with model monitoring, drift detection, performance dashboards, A/B testing infrastructure, and continuous model retraining pipelines.

## 12. FAQs

**What types of custom AI applications can you build?**
We build computer vision systems, NLP engines, recommendation platforms, generative AI tools, analytics dashboards, decision support systems, and any purpose-built AI application that solves a specific business problem.

**Do we need our own data to build a custom AI application?**
It depends on the use case. Some applications work well with pre-trained models and RAG pipelines. Others require domain-specific fine-tuning with your data. We assess your data during the discovery phase and recommend the optimal approach.

**How do you ensure AI model accuracy?**
We define evaluation benchmarks during the problem definition phase and test rigorously — precision, recall, F1 scores, and domain-specific metrics. We iterate on model architecture, training data, and prompt engineering until benchmarks are met.

**Do you handle ongoing model maintenance?**
Yes. We build monitoring infrastructure for model drift detection, set up automated retraining pipelines, and provide ongoing optimization support for deployed AI applications.
