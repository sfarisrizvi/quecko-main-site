## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Your teams are spending hours reading documents that machines should handle in seconds.

### Body
Enterprises process thousands of invoices, contracts, applications, and forms every week — many still handled by humans manually entering data into spreadsheets and ERP systems. OCR alone doesn't solve this — scanned documents have variable layouts, handwriting, poor scan quality, and domain-specific terminology that generic extraction tools butcher. What's needed is an intelligent system that understands document structure, adapts to format variations, extracts data with high confidence, validates against business rules, and routes results to the right system — with human review only for edge cases. Quecko builds IDP pipelines that do exactly this.

## 3. CORE CAPABILITIES

### Headline
Comprehensive IDP Engineering

### Grid

- **Multi-Format Document Extraction:** AI-powered extraction from PDFs, scanned images, photographs, and digital documents. We handle invoices, purchase orders, contracts, receipts, medical records, and government forms — across variable layouts and languages.

- **Document Classification & Routing:** Automatic classification of incoming documents by type, urgency, department, or workflow — routing to the correct processing pipeline or human reviewer without manual triage.

- **Handwriting & Degraded Scan Processing:** Advanced OCR combined with AI post-processing for handwritten text, low-quality scans, skewed documents, and multi-language content — recovering data that basic OCR misses.

- **Validation & Business Rule Engine:** Extracted data is validated against your business rules, cross-referenced with existing records, and flagged for anomalies — catching errors before they enter your systems.

- **Human-in-the-Loop Review:** Confidence-based routing that sends low-confidence extractions to human reviewers — with pre-filled fields, highlighted discrepancies, and one-click approval to minimize review time.

- **System Integration & Data Routing:** Extracted data is automatically pushed to your ERP, CRM, accounting software, or custom databases — via API, direct database writes, or file-based transfer.

## 12. FAQs

**What document types can your IDP system handle?**
Invoices, purchase orders, contracts, receipts, insurance claims, medical records, government forms, bank statements, and any structured or semi-structured document. We adapt to your specific document formats during training.

**How accurate is the extraction?**
We target 95%+ extraction accuracy on production data. For fields where accuracy is critical (amounts, dates, identifiers), we set higher confidence thresholds and route uncertain extractions to human review.

**Can IDP integrate with our existing ERP/accounting system?**
Yes. We build integrations with SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, Xero, Salesforce, and custom enterprise systems — pushing extracted data directly into your workflows without manual re-entry.

**Does the system improve over time?**
Yes. Human corrections during the review process feed back into model retraining — the system learns from its mistakes and continuously improves extraction accuracy.
