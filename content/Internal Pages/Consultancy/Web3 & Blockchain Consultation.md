## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
Blockchain adoption decisions increasingly happen at the board or executive level, often driven by competitive pressure without a clear internal technical view of what's actually feasible, compliant, and cost-effective. Getting an unbiased outside assessment before committing budget avoids the two most common failure modes: building something that didn't need blockchain at all, or under-scoping a genuinely valuable use case.

### Body
Not every business problem needs blockchain, and not every blockchain vendor will tell a client that. Organizations need a partner whose recommendation isn't automatically "yes, build it" — someone who will say no when a traditional database solves the problem better and faster.
