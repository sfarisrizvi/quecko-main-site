## 12. FAQs

**How do you guarantee that user credentials are not compromised?**
We integrate directly with Identity Providers via OAuth 2.0 / SAML. The app never handles or stores user passwords directly. Authentication tokens are saved in the device's hardware-backed secure enclave (Keychain/Keystore) and expire automatically.

**How does offline data synchronization work?**
The app saves transaction data locally in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) when offline. When internet is detected, the app pushes the changes to a staging API. The backend compares timestamps and data schemas, resolving sync conflicts based on predefined business rules before updating the core ERP.

**Can you deploy apps securely to devices we don't own (BYOD)?**
Yes. By integrating with Mobile Application Management (MAM) policies inside platforms like Microsoft Intune, we can secure corporate data inside our app even on personal devices, preventing copy-pasting to non-managed apps.

**How do we push app updates to a fleet of field devices?**
We configure the application for private distribution through enterprise app catalogs, Google Play Private Channel, or directly through your MDM dashboard, allowing IT admins to push updates silently in the background.
