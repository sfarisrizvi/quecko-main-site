## 2. THE CHALLENGE

### Headline
If your mobile shopping app is just a website wrapper, users will abandon their carts.

### Body
Mobile represents over 70% of online shopping traffic, yet mobile conversion rates are notoriously lower than desktop. The reason: slow page loads, clunky navigation, and complicated checkout forms on small screens. Pushing users to a responsive website or a slow app wrapper results in high bounce rates and lost revenue. To capture today's shoppers, you need a mobile application that loads catalogs instantly, supports single-tap payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and keeps users engaged through targeted, non-intrusive push notifications. Quecko builds native and optimized cross-platform e-commerce applications with performance budgets. We prioritize checkout speed, image caching, and localized catalog loading to deliver fluid shopping experiences.

## 12. FAQs

**Can the mobile app sync directly with our existing Shopify inventory?**
Yes. We integrate with Shopify's Storefront API (GraphQL). This allows us to fetch live product details, variant allocations, pricing changes, and customer profiles in real-time, ensuring inventory count parity between web and mobile.

**How do you protect customer transaction data inside the app?**
We use tokenized payment architectures (via Stripe or Adyen). The card information is encrypted directly on input and sent directly to the gateway's server. Our application database and mobile client only save a secure payment token, keeping your app compliant with PCI standards.

**What is the advantage of a headless mobile app setup?**
In a headless setup, the frontend mobile application is decoupled from the backend CMS. This allows the mobile app to communicate with lightweight database APIs directly, yielding faster catalog load speeds and more flexible UI options than standard web layouts.

**How do we target users who add items to their cart but don't buy?**
We integrate push notification triggers linked to your analytics database. If a user adds an item to their cart and does not checkout within a set timeframe (e.g., 30 minutes), the system automatically triggers a personalized push alert with a call to action.
