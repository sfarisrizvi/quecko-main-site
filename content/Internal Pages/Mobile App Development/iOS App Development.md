## 12. FAQs

**Why choose native Swift over cross-platform options like Flutter?**
Native Swift gives you absolute control over performance, animations, and memory usage. It ensures zero lag, full access to new Apple features on day one, and a UX that matches Apple's guidelines perfectly. If your app requires complex graphics, heavy background processing, or premium UI polish, native Swift is the optimal choice.

**How long does Apple's app review process take?**
Typically 24 to 48 hours, though it can take longer if Apple raises questions regarding data privacy, subscriptions, or requires clarification on specific features. We minimize this time by ensuring absolute compliance before submission.

**Can you integrate Apple Pay into our app?**
Yes. We integrate Apple Pay natively using Apple's PassKit framework, linking it securely to your payment gateway (Stripe, Adyen) to ensure frictionless, single-touch transactions.

**How do you test the app before it goes live?**
We use Apple's TestFlight to distribute beta builds to internal and external testers. We track crashes and performance data in real-time, ensuring all major issues are resolved before the public App Store release.
