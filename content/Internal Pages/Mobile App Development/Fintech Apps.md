## 12. FAQs

**How do you ensure PCI-DSS compliance in a mobile app?**
We use payment SDKs (like Stripe or Adyen) that handle card input via secure tokenization. The card data goes directly from the user's input fields to the payment processor's servers. Our mobile client and backend only receive a secure token, ensuring we do not store, process, or transmit raw credit card details.

**How do you prevent data leaks on lost or rooted/jailbroken devices?**
We implement jailbreak and root detection at app startup, preventing execution if the OS has been compromised. All local cached data is encrypted using SQLCipher with keys generated inside hardware enclaves that require biometric verification to access.

**What is the role of Plaid in a fintech application?**
Plaid allows users to securely connect their bank accounts to your app. It handles login verification natively and returns transactional data and account details to your backend securely, without your application needing to see their bank passwords.

**Can you build crypto/Web3 transaction layers inside the app?**
Yes. We have deep experience building Web3 systems. We can integrate multi-chain crypto wallets, smart contract interactions, token swaps, and fiat-crypto rails directly alongside traditional fintech systems.
