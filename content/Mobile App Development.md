Without expert mobile engineering, you risk wasting budget on apps that fail store reviews and turn customers away.

## 10. OBJECTION HANDLING / FAQ

**Q1. What mobile app development services does Quecko offer?**
Quecko specializes in native iOS and Android development, cross-platform app creation (Flutter and React Native), enterprise mobile systems, fintech apps, e-commerce apps, super apps, and ongoing app maintenance.

**Q2. Should we build a native or a cross-platform (Flutter/React Native) mobile app?**
If your app requires heavy graphic rendering, complex device sensor integrations, or strict platform-specific UI patterns, native is ideal. For content-driven, e-commerce, or SaaS apps seeking faster time-to-market and lower costs, cross-platform is highly recommended.

**Q3. Does Quecko handle the submission process to the App Store and Google Play?**
Yes. We manage the entire store submission process, including metadata configuration, asset preparation, review management, and post-launch updates.

**Q4. How does the resource augmentation process work for mobile app projects?**
We assess your software stack and timeline, match you with top-tier mobile developers, onboard them seamlessly, and support them as they integrate with your core team.
