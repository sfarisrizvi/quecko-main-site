## 10. OBJECTION HANDLING / FAQ

**Q1. What e-commerce and CMS services does Quecko offer?**
Quecko specializes in custom Shopify development, Shopify Plus enterprise stores, WooCommerce development, WordPress site creation, headless commerce implementations, multi-vendor marketplaces, custom e-commerce apps, payment gateway integrations, and conversion rate optimization (CRO).

**Q2. Why should we choose a Headless Commerce setup over standard templates?**
Headless commerce separates your customer-facing frontend from your backend commerce engine. This allows for near-instant page load speeds, complete design freedom, and the ability to sell across multiple frontends (web, mobile, smart devices) using the same backend catalog.

**Q3. Can Quecko migrate our existing catalog and customer history to a new platform?**
Yes. We perform secure migrations, transferring product catalogs, customer databases, order histories, and SEO redirects from your legacy platform to your new storefront without downtime.

**Q4. How does the resource augmentation process work for e-commerce developers?**
We assess your software stack and timeline, match you with top-tier frontend or full-stack developers, onboard them seamlessly, and support them as they integrate with your core team.
