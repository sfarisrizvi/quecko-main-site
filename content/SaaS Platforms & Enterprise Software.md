## 10. OBJECTION HANDLING / FAQ

**Q1. What custom SaaS and enterprise software services does Quecko offer?**
Quecko specializes in building custom CRM solutions, ERP systems, HRM platforms, LMS platforms, retail management software, white-label SaaS development, subscription and billing engines, multi-tenant SaaS architecture, and business intelligence dashboards.

**Q2. How does Quecko ensure database isolation and security in multi-tenant SaaS?**
We architect clean database schemas using either database-per-tenant or schema-level isolation strategies. Coupled with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), we ensure tenant data is completely isolated and secure.

**Q3. Can Quecko integrate with our existing legacy systems and APIs?**
Yes. We design custom middleware and secure REST or GraphQL APIs to connect your new platform with legacy databases, internal APIs, or external business tools.

**Q4. How does the resource augmentation process work for enterprise software projects?**
We assess your software stack and timeline, match you with top-tier full-stack developers, designers, or database engineers, onboard them seamlessly, and support them as they integrate with your core team.

**Q5. How do we get started with Quecko?**
Getting started is simple. Contact us via our website or email (Sales@Quecko.com). We will schedule a technical consultation to map out your requirements and recommend the best roadmap for your project.
